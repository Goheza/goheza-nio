### Changes from the original draft
- "Turn your creativity into money" is now "into income". It reads less like a get-rich promise, which App Review tends to push back on.
- Bold-style lines are now `•` bullets. App Store descriptions are plain text, so bullets display more cleanly.
- Added the last line saying earnings depend on availability, approval and performance. Apps that make earning claims often get rejected under Guideline 2.3 (accurate metadata), and this line helps avoid that. Keep it.

## App Review Notes (important)

The app needs a login, so give Apple **a demo creator account (email + password)** in the "Sign-in required" section. Sample campaigns should already be visible in it.

Suggested note:

> Goheza connects creators with businesses for paid content campaigns. Creators are paid by businesses for real-world marketing services, so payouts are not in-app purchases. Use the demo account to browse campaigns, apply, and view the earnings dashboard.

This heads off a common rejection where reviewers think creator payouts should go through Apple's in-app purchases (Guideline 3.1). If businesses can pay for campaigns inside the iOS app, explain that it's a payment for advertising services.
