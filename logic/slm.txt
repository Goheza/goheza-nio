Here is the whole process of the application just in case your are not sure.

the app has three entities the brand, Admin and creator.

Then Admin:
The admin is available and he can be a brand or a creator, that is he can be available through the admin talb.

roles:
 - verify brands
 - verify campaigns 
 - suspend creators 
 - Post videos to tiktok

INTIALLY:
 - When the brand arrived in there dashboard they get a birds eye view of everything happening, number of campaigns, brands, creators, and pending submissions

 - So when they go to the brand tab, they get to verify the brand which updates their brand_profiles table.

 - So when the admin also goes to the campaigns tab, they get to verify which campaign should be available to the creators
 
 - The Admin can also suspend creators (still debatable about this)
 
 - Now the campaign can post these submissions that were verified by the brand through the campaign_submissions table  to tiktok and the posted items and the posted 
    retrieved API data(from tiktok is returned) this data is stored in the campaign_posts,which has the video id, media_id, platform, status whether posted or not,

  -  So these campaign_posts are then used to retrieve data from tiktok (the Analytics) for each creator and are stored in campaign_insights

The brand:
The brand is the major one in this situation. Now the brand has accounts and brands exist in the brand_profile table when they create an account, the brand has now the following roles.
 - Create campaigns
 - accept creator Applications
 - accept creator submissions.
 - view analytics of the one going campaigns they have separately

So when the brand visits his dashboard, he expected to see his active campaigns, available creator applications, available creator submissions.

INTIALLY:
 - The brand creates a campaign because he would like Goheza as a platform to do performance marketing for his brand he makes this campaign with all the necessary 
    things they want and what should exist, afterwards, they submit this campaign, through the campaigns table, this campaign first goes through the Admin to actually 
    be verified , after the campaign has been verified by the admin, it goes forward to the creators,

 - After that the brand is expecting to get back a campaign applications through the campaign_applications table, which he views the use of this is to enable creators
    show their interest in a campaign, this is done through the campaign_applications table, when that is complete the brand has to either verify or decline the creators
   application based on what they will see in the application.
 
 - On Accepting the campaign_application, the brand expects a campaign_submission containing a video from the creator, which comes through the campaign_submissions
    table, at that moment, that moment the brand verifies the campaign_submission from the creator , the submission is moved forward to the Admin.

 - Now in the Analyics part of the brand, they are expected to see analytics based around a posted video, that belonged to a campaign_submission from a creator, who was
    verified by the brand through their campaign_application. so they see the analytics and the analytics data is stored in campaign_insights, in this way they get live
    live data but also stored in the database.


The Creator:
The Creator has the following roles:
 - Apply for a campaign
 - Send a Submission.


INTIALLY:
    - The Creator goes to Goheza platform because he wants to participate in the performance marketing so he signs up and its stored in creator_profiles,   

    - But in the process they also add there social media data which is stored in the creator_social_accounts table. This enables us to gather some info about them 
      that we hope to include in the campaign_application that would be available to the brand when they wish to approve the creator for a submission

    - So the creator goes to the campaigns table expecting to see campaigns that are available that were posted by the brand, through the campaigns table, and when they
    see these they apply through the campaign_applications table to show there interest in a pariticular campaign,

    -  When there application is verified they now can actually make a submission through the campaign_submission table to that very campaign, which included adding
    there video to the campaign_submission, which we actually store.Of course when not verified they are given back a rejection reason.

    - At this point the creator is done with what we need them to do.
